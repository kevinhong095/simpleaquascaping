My Top 21 Fish

Over the years, I've tried pretty much every type of nano fish out there — and I did it intentionally. I wanted firsthand experience with all of it: the best schooling fish, the best fish for cleaning, the best centerpiece fish. All of that takes time to try, keep, and actually learn from.

Nearing a decade deep into aquascaping, I've been lucky enough to keep hundreds of different fish.

Here are my personal favorites:

My All-Time Top 5

If I could only keep 5 — these are the ones. Tab through 3 layout options below.

1
Celestial Pearl Danios

I fell in love with these the first time I saw them. Fully grown, the midnight-blue body gets this incredible iridescence, set off by pearly white spots and deep reddish-orange fins. Males will even spar with each other for a female's attention — the behavior alone makes them worth keeping.

2
Rummy Nose Tetra

These are my mom's favorite fish, so you'll always find them somewhere in my personal gallery. I love the slender body, the fin shape, the bright red tip on their nose, and the bold black-and-white striping on their caudal fin. On top of all that, they're the tightest-schooling fish I've ever kept — an extraordinarily tight, cohesive shoal that moves as one.

3
Pygmy Cory

Arguably my favorite for behavior. They waddle and float in place like little helicopters, occasionally darting up to the surface for a quick gulp of air. An underrated schooling fish, too — watching a large pack move together, shaking their bodies in sync, is genuinely incredible to watch.

4
Scarlet Badis

These fish do not get enough love. Fed properly, males show off metallic red stripes running down their bodies and bright blue front fins — color that rivals anything in a reef tank.

5
Bloody Mary Shrimp

So red they practically glow. Scattered throughout the tank — especially hanging out on plants — they add little pops of color everywhere you look, and bring a lot of life into the tank.
