My Top 20 Fish

Over the years, I've tried pretty much every type of nano fish out there — and I did it intentionally. I wanted firsthand experience with all of it: the best schooling fish, the best cleanup crew, the best centerpiece fish. All of that takes time to try, keep, and actually learn from.

Nearing a decade deep into aquascaping, I've been lucky enough to keep hundreds of different fish.

Here are my personal favorites:

My All-Time Top 5

If I could only keep 5 — these are the ones.

#1
Celestial Pearl Danios

Covered in pearl-like spots, with fins that glow orange under the right light. My all-time favorite, no contest.

#2
Rummy Nose Tetra

The tell-tale sign of a healthy tank — their nose flushes bright red when the water's right.

#3
Pygmy Cory

Tiny bottom-dwelling clowns that never stop foraging. Impossible not to smile at.

#4
Scarlet Badis

Small but ridiculously vivid — a tiny reef fish trapped in a freshwater body.

#5
Bloody Mary Shrimp

Solid red from head to tail — the easiest way to add color without adding another fish.
