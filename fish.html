What Livestock to Buy

Over the years, I've tried pretty much every type of nano fish out there — and I did it intentionally. I wanted firsthand experience with all of it: the best schooling fish, the best fish for cleaning, the best centerpiece fish. All of that takes time to try, keep, and actually learn from.

Nearing a decade deep into aquascaping, I've been lucky enough to keep hundreds of different fish.

There are a lot of great nano fish out there, but that doesn't mean they're all worth personally recommending. Below is my personal, curated list of favorites.

My All-Time Top 5

If I could only keep 5 — these are the ones.

1
Celestial Pearl Danios

I fell in love with these the first time I saw them. Fully grown, the midnight-blue body gets this incredible iridescence, set off by pearly white spots and deep red fins. Males will spar with each other for a female's attention, and you'll see couples break off and stay together.

2
Rummy Nose Tetra

These are my mom's favorite fish, so I always have a school of them somewhere in my personal aquascaping gallery. I really love their body and fin shapes — especially the red tip on their nose and the black-and-white striping on their tails. On top of all that, they're the tightest-schooling fish I've ever kept.

3
Pygmy Cory

Arguably my favorite for behavior. They waddle and float in place like little helicopters, occasionally darting up to the surface for a quick gulp of air. An underrated schooling fish, too — watching a large pack move together, shaking their bodies in sync, is genuinely incredible to watch.

4
Scarlet Badis

These are among some of the most underrated fish in the entire hobby. When they're fully colored up, males show deep red stripes running down their bodies — coloring up to an extraordinarily intense red — with bright, metallic cyan-blue front fins.

5
Bloody Mary Shrimp

So red they practically glow. Scattered throughout the tank — especially hanging out on plants — they add little pops of color everywhere you look, and bring a lot of life into the tank.
