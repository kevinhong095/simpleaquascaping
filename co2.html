CO2 Setup & Schedule

CO2 is the single best thing you can add to a planted tank early. It's not optional if you're serious about growth — and it won't stress your fish if you dial it in right.

Best Practices for CO2

  • Use an inline CO2 diffuser. It injects CO2 directly into your filter outflow — more efficient than in-tank diffusers and keeps your tank visually cleaner.
  • Run CO2 as high as your tank can safely handle. This helps prevent and control algae. In most cases, that's roughly 2–4 BPS.
  • I don't use drop checkers — they're slow and misleading. I push CO2 as high as possible. If fish are fine, you're fine.

How to Dial It In

  1. 1
    Start at 1 bubble per second (BPS).
  2. 2
    Increase by +1 BPS every 3 days.

Watch your fish

If they begin gasping at the surface → stop CO2 for the day. The next day, resume at the last stable BPS.
