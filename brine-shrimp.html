Weekly Routine

Live Baby Brine Shrimp Setup

Few things garner compliments as much as healthy, vibrant, active fish. Live food isn't just one of the experiences to have with your tank — it's the most consistent way to the best color, breeding, and behavior.

No other live food is 60% protein and this easy to take care of. Set it up once, harvest every morning.

What You Need

🌀

Brine Shrimp Hatchery
🌬️

USB Air Pump
🧂

Aquarium Salt

🦐

Baby Brine Shrimp Eggs

The Recipe

2L
Tap water — no dechlorinator. Chlorine helps break down egg casings.
2 tbsp
Aquarium salt — about 1 tbsp per liter.
¼–1 tsp
Brine shrimp eggs — scale up to 1–2 tbsp daily for 20+ tanks.

Hatching Process

  1. 1
    Fill with tap water and turn on the bubbler.
  2. 2
    Add salt.
  3. 3
    Add eggs.

Timing depends on temperature

Average wait time is 36 hours at standard room temperature.

How to Harvest the Brine Shrimp

  1. 1
    Turn off the air pump.
  2. 2
    Wait 5–10 minutes for the eggshells to float — live nauplii will be floating in the water column.
  3. 3
    Drain the hatchery through a brine shrimp net — it lets the salt water out but keeps the live shrimp in.
  4. 4
    Stop about 2cm before the floating eggshells at the top get pulled into the drain.
  5. 5
    Feed using a syringe or turkey baster into your tanks.
