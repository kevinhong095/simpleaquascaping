Live Baby Brine Shrimp Setup

A continuous supply of fresh food your fish will actually go crazy for — set up once, harvest every morning.

What You Need

🧪

Cone Hatchery (~2L)

Z-Hatcher or similar. The cone shape keeps eggs suspended and improves hatch rates.
💨

USB Air Pump

Position below the hatchery. Rigid tubing at the bottom keeps eggs moving.
💡

LED Light

Warms the water slightly and attracts nauplii to one spot at harvest time.

🥚

Quality Eggs

Store in a sealed metal can in the fridge. Good eggs hatch 95%+. Cheap eggs don't.

The Recipe

2L
Tap water — no dechlorinator. Chlorine helps break down egg casings.
2 tbsp
Aquarium salt — about 1 tbsp per liter.
¼–1 tsp
Brine shrimp eggs — scale up to 1–2 tbsp daily for 20+ tanks.

Hatch It

Set it up in the evening. Harvest the next morning.

  1. 1
    Fill with tap water.
  2. 2
    Add salt.
  3. 3
    Dissolve.
  4. 4
    Add eggs.
  5. 5
    Run continuous aeration.
  6. 6
    Wait ~36 hours.

Timing depends on temperature

Hot room = 24h, cold room = 48h. Target temp ~78°F — the LED provides enough warmth.

Harvest

Turn it off. Wait. Drain the good stuff.

  1. 1
    Turn off the air.
  2. 2
    Wait 5–10 minutes.
  3. 3
    Egg shells float, live nauplii sink.
  4. 4
    Drain from the bottom valve into a clear container.
  5. 5
    Stop when the water runs clear — "liquid gold."

Keep it going

Running two hatchers on offset schedules gives you a daily supply without gaps.
